\# SALORIS – NASA Research Source Register



These sources inform the educational content, health-risk context and research data used in SALORIS.



\## 1. NASA Human Research Program – Five Hazards



\*\*Source:\*\* NASA – Five Hazards of Human Spaceflight  

https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/



\*\*Use in SALORIS:\*\* Background for the five hazards of human spaceflight:

\- Space radiation

\- Isolation and confinement

\- Distance from Earth

\- Gravity fields

\- Hostile and closed environments



\## 2. NASA Human Research Program – Human Spaceflight Risks



\*\*Source:\*\* NASA – Risks of Human Spaceflight  

https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/



\*\*Use in SALORIS:\*\* Research context for physiological, behavioural and radiation-related risks to astronaut health.



\## 3. NASA Open Science Data Repository – OSD-575



\*\*Source:\*\* NASA OSDR – OSD-575  

https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-575



\*\*Use in SALORIS:\*\* Inspiration4 study data used for dataset inspection, preprocessing, individual baseline analysis and experimental anomaly analysis.



\*\*Data limitations:\*\* The dataset includes a small number of participants and cannot establish generalisable clinical thresholds or support medical diagnosis. SALORIS treats the resulting ML analysis as experimental.



\## 4. NASA Human Research Program



\*\*Source:\*\* NASA Human Research Program  

https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/



\*\*Use in SALORIS:\*\* General research background on protecting astronaut health and performance during spaceflight.



\## 5. NASA Analog Missions



\*\*Source:\*\* NASA Analog Missions  

https://www.nasa.gov/analog-missions/



\*\*Use in SALORIS:\*\* Background on ground-based analog environments used to study challenges associated with human space exploration.
